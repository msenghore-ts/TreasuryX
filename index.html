For Providers

Access

Get in the room with the people you're building for.

If you are building for treasury teams (a TMS, a cash investing platform, an FX tool, a working capital product) and you have struggled to get in front of real practitioners, we can help. Carefully.

  • Structured feedback sessions with active, verified practitioners across 28 countries
  • Insight into the real workflows your product needs to fit
  • Candid written feedback: what worked, what did not, and what they would ask next
  • Practitioners are compensated for their time, so the feedback is serious
Credibility

Build credibility before you enter the market.

The treasury market is tight-knit. Reputation travels fast. Getting your product in front of the right people, on their terms, through a trusted intermediary, is worth more than any marketing campaign.

  • Access to 450+ treasury decision-makers across 28 countries
  • Warm follow-ups, opt-in only: if a practitioner is interested, we make the introduction
  • Partners apply. Not every provider is a fit, and we will tell you honestly if there is not one
